Tap an animal lineage to replay its most prolific member in the 3D view, or a plant lineage to see its largest individual full-grown. Plants and animals share one graph genome: capsule nodes are limbs or stems, membranes are wings or leaves, pads are feet or roots. Life starts in the sea as floating algae sheets and animals that can only graze them; predation, scavenging, stems, roots and land tolerance all evolve. A motility gene decides kingdom: offspring above 0.5 are born motile (animals), below it sessile (plants), so lineages can cross over in either direction. Plants reach land through a land-tolerance gene and the wet shore; herbivores follow because shore-dwellers can graze adjacent land plants from the water, better the more they can crawl, and every new body is measured on land as well as in water. Species are lineages: a child with a changed body plan starts a new lineage with some probability.

How the physics ties to the dials
  • Gravity: weight grows with volume × g; muscle torque with limb cross-section. Heavy worlds favour short, thick limbs.
  • Pressure, O₂, CO₂, temperature set air density by the ideal gas law. Every membrane sub-panel gets a flat-plate normal force F = ½ρA|v|²·2.61·sin α plus parasitic drag; limbs get anisotropic quadratic drag.
  • O₂ partial pressure feeds an oxygen budget: lung fraction × torso volume × pO₂ against muscle section × frequency. The ratio scales motor strength (fatigue factor).
  • Humidity and temperature set aridity. Land fitness is multiplied by hydration unless the water-store gene covers the need; stored water adds mass.
  • Water environment adds Archimedes buoyancy from displaced volume and submersion damping; the same lift code runs at water density, so fins and wings share physics.
  • Scoring: Land = distance walked in 8 s after a 1 s settle. Water = distance swum. Air = dropped from 10 m, distance at landing + 0.25 × seconds aloft.
  • Everything runs at a fixed 120 Hz step. Runs that go non-finite or exceed 60 m/s score zero, because evolution exploits any energy leak.
